OPEN WALLET secure integrated build

Features added in this build:
- TXID/network/recipient/token-contract/amount verification
- Verified-deposit balance refresh and USD valuation
- Active device/session tracking, approval and revocation
- Sensitive Send/Swap protection for new/untrusted sessions
- Persistent transaction history with sent-coin details
- Incoming external deposit maximum is unlimited; $0.10 minimum remains
- Outgoing Send has no application-layer maximum; $0.10 USD-equivalent minimum remains
- Failed/reverted transactions are not credited

- Outgoing Send network fee: 0.5% of amount, charged in selected asset/network; released only after recipient transfer verification.
